Quote on Site

Find the right fence. Give a defensible price. In 90 seconds.

Why this exists

In fencing, whoever puts a number in front of the customer first, sets the anchor. If you visit a site and say "I'll send you the quote tomorrow," the customer has 24–48 hours to talk to a competitor who quoted on the spot. By the time your quote lands, you're not pitching — you're arguing against a number someone else already put in their head.

This tool does two things: it helps the customer pick the right fence from JustFence's 20 products, and it lets you quote that fence on the spot — with a total built from real cost components, not a guess.

What it does
  • Find Your Fence — four quick questions recommend the right fence from the 20-product range, with two alternates.
  • Quote on the spot — pick a product, enter dimensions, and the rate is derived from a cost model of mesh, posts, footings, fittings and labour.
  • Every quote shows its build-up — the customer sees exactly how ₹285 per sq ft is composed. Nothing is a black box.
  • Override with proportional redistribution — if the site needs a different rate, enter the new number and every component scales by the same factor.
  • One tap sends a formatted WhatsApp — ready for the customer to read in 5 seconds.
  • One tap prints a slip — for the customer's site file.
What it is not
  • Not a replacement for the formal quotation. The office still sends the full quote with number, address, GSTIN, terms, validity.
  • Not a final commitment. Tell the customer: "This is an indicative price. The formal quotation follows after site verification."
  • Not a material order. It doesn't calculate posts, clips or concrete bags — that's the office configurator's job.
  • Not a sign-off on rates. Until the office replaces the placeholder cost model with JustFence's actual rates, the header badge says "Indicative rates".
How to use it
1
If the customer isn't sure what they need, tap "Help me choose" and answer four questions. You'll get a recommendation and two alternates.
2
If you already know the product, tap "I know the product" and pick it directly.
3
Enter length and height from your site measurement. Total updates live.
4
Set the site condition — Level, Sloping, Rocky, or Wall-mounted.
5
Override only if needed — the build-up still explains the total, proportionally scaled.
6
Tap WhatsApp to send the quote to the customer before you leave the gate.
Why on-the-spot matters

A quote you send tomorrow is a quote the customer may read. A quote you put in their hand today is a quote they will react to — and once they've reacted, you're in a conversation, not a queue.
